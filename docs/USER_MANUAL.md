# How to use BehavioralTracker

## The main steps
1. **Experiment** — give the experiment a name, add your videos and type the Animal ID and group of each video.
2. **Video Setup** — choose the test (open field, elevated plus maze, light/dark box, Y / T maze, novel object,
   three-chamber or your own) and put the zones on the video: **⚡ Auto** finds the apparatus, **🎯 Guided** asks
   you to click a few corners, or draw the zones yourself. Several cages in one video: **▦ Chambers**.
3. **Tracking** — check the animal colour (darker or lighter than the floor) and press **🎯 Auto-set contrast**.
   Press **Test detection** to see whether the animal is found, then **▶ Start Tracking**.
4. **Results** — the track, heat map, zone times and distances open automatically. Every video also gets an
   Excel workbook, CSV files, charts and a one-page PDF summary in its results folder.

**Many videos:** **Batch Processing** runs the same zones and settings on all of them and makes one summary table.

**Simple view** (tick box at the bottom of the side bar) shows only the pages most people need. Untick it to see
all pages.

## Live tracking with a camera
Tracking page ▸ **📷 Live tracking from a camera**. Choose the camera, put the zones on the camera picture,
press **Capture empty arena** before you put the animal in, then start the trial. The trial is recorded and
tracked at the same time; the results are ready right after the trial.

## Freezing
1. **Freezing – Individual** ▸ open a video.
2. Draw the **⬠ Region**: click the corners of the chamber floor; double-click or press Enter when done.
3. **▨ Mask** anything that moves but is not the animal (reflections in clear walls, a lamp, a cable).
4. Press **✨ Suggest** (animal colour, contrast and size) and **🎯 Test detection** to check that the animal is
   found. **📏 Measure** the animal (nose to tail base) for the most accurate result.
5. Add the **epochs** of your protocol (baseline, tones, ITI): **＋ Add**, **＋ Tones…** for regular tones, or
   **⤒ Import…** a table of start and end times. Every epoch gets its own freezing result.
6. Press **❄ Analyse this video.**

Below the result BT lists anything that could make the number wrong: ⛔ = fix this before using the number,
⚠ = please check, ℹ = information. Several animals in separate chambers: **▦ Chambers ▸ Add a chamber**.
Many videos with the same settings: **Freezing – Batch**. **☰ Presets** saves your settings to use again or to
share with your lab.

## Event Scoring
Add the behaviours you want to score, each with a key (＋ Add), play the video and press the keys.
**📊 Plots & analysis** shows the ethogram, time budget and agreement between two observers.
**🧠 Classifier** learns a behaviour (for example grooming or rearing) from videos you scored by hand and then finds it
in new videos.

## Statistics
**Analysis** ▸ Statistics compares groups and time points and tells you which test was used and why.

## If something goes wrong
* **The animal is lost, or a reflection is tracked** — mask the reflections (▨ Mask), set the animal colour,
  press ✨ Suggest or 🎯 Auto-set contrast, 📏 measure the animal, and test at a few frames.
* **Live tracking loses the animal** — capture the empty arena before the animal is put in, and keep the camera
  and the light still during the trial.
* **"The program files have been changed"** at start — an antivirus program changed or removed a file of
  BehavioralTracker. Restore it from the antivirus quarantine (or add the BehavioralTracker folder to its
  exceptions) and run the Setup again; your settings and results are kept.
